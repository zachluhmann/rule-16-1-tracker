INITIAL PROCEDURE ORDER NO. 1

This Order shall, unless superseded by subsequent orders, govern the procedure in all
actions transferred to this Court by the Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation
pursuant to its Orders of June 9, 2026 and June 18, 2026, all actions pending in the
District of Kansas that were deemed included in the MDL pursuant to this Court's Order of
July 7, 2026, as well as any related or "tag-along" actions originally filed in this Court
or transferred or removed to this Court. Those actions are listed in Exhibit A attached to
this Order.

1. CONSOLIDATION—The civil actions listed above are consolidated for pretrial purposes.
Any "tag-along actions" filed in, removed to, or transferred to this Court shall be
automatically consolidated with this action without the necessity of future motions or
orders. This consolidation does not constitute a determination that the actions should be
consolidated for trial, nor does it have the effect of making any entity a party to any
action in which that party has not been named, served, or added in accordance with the
Federal Rules of Civil Procedure. Any party
[Page 1 of 10]
-2-
that objects to such consolidation or to any other provision of this Order must raise that
objection at the Initial Management Conference (to be set by this Order) or, if the case is
consolidated following that conference, such party must raise the objection by a motion for
relief within ten (10) days of the earlier of counsel's first appearance herein or the entry
of a consolidation order in such case.

2. CORPORATE DISCLOSURE STATEMENTS—No later than August 28, 2026, each party must update
its corporate disclosure statements as Fed. R. Civ. P. 7.1 requires. Additionally, the
parties must identify all parent, subsidiary, or affiliate entities that have outstanding
securities in the hands of the public.

3. DATE OF INITIAL MANAGEMENT CONFERENCE—Matters relating to pretrial and discovery
proceedings in these cases will be addressed at the Initial Management Conference, which is
hereby set for September 22, 2026, at 9:30 a.m. in Courtroom 408 of the Wichita U.S. Federal
Court, 401 N. Market, Wichita, Kansas. Each party represented by counsel must appear at the
initial scheduling conference through an attorney in person or remotely who will bear
primary responsibility for the party's interest in this litigation. Attendance at the
conference will not waive objections to jurisdiction, venue, or service. All counsel shall
notify the Court three days in advance of the conference whether they will appear in person
or will participate remotely.

4. AGENDA FOR INITIAL MANAGEMENT CONFERENCE—The items listed in Federal Rule of Civil
Procedure 16.1, as well as sections 11.21, 11.211, 11.212, 11.213, and 11.214 of the Manual
for Complex Litigation, Fourth ("MCL 4th") shall, to the extent applicable, constitute a
tentative agenda for the Initial Management Conference. Counsel are expected to familiarize
themselves with Rule 16.1 and MCL 4th prior to the Initial Management Conference
[Page 2 of 10]
-3-
and to be prepared to suggest procedures that will facilitate the expeditious, economical,
and just resolution of this litigation.

5. MEET AND CONFER PRIOR TO INITIAL MANAGEMENT CONFERENCE—Counsel shall—by not later than
August 14, 2026—meet, confer, and seek consensus to the extent possible about items on the
Initial Management Conference agenda, including a proposed discovery plan, amendment of
pleadings, and consideration of procedures for resolving class action allegations and
motions.

     vi.  any limits on activity by nonleadership counsel; and

7. SUMMARY OF FACTUAL AND LEGAL ISSUES—Plaintiffs and Defendants must submit to
ksd_melgren_chambers@ksd.uscourts.gov and ksd_severson_chambers@ksd.uscourts.gov not later
than September 4, 2026, a brief written statement which states their preliminary
understanding of the facts involved in the litigation and the critical factual and legal
issues. These statements will not be filed with the Clerk, will not be binding, will not
waive claims or defenses, and may not be offered in evidence against a party at later
proceedings. The court limits the parties to one submission for all Plaintiffs and one
submission for all Defendants.

8. PROPOSED INITIAL MANAGEMENT ORDER—Following the Initial Management Conference, the
parties shall jointly prepare and submit to the Court a proposed initial management order by
not later than October 23, 2026.

9. PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE—All parties and their counsel are directed to preserve evidence
that may be relevant to this action. The duty extends to documents, data, and tangible
things in possession, custody, and control of the parties to this action, and any employees,
agents, contractors, carriers, bailees, or other nonparties who possess materials reasonably
anticipated to be subject to discovery in this action. "Documents, data, and tangible
things" is to be interpreted broadly. Information that serves to identify, locate, or link
such material, such as file inventories, file folders, indices, and metadata, is also
included in this definition. Preservation includes the obligation not to alter any such
thing as to its form, content, or manner of filing.

Until the parties reach an agreement on a preservation plan or the Court orders otherwise,
each party shall take reasonable steps to preserve all documents, data, and tangible things
containing information potentially relevant to the subject matter of this litigation. Each
counsel is under an obligation to the Court to exercise all reasonable efforts to identify
and notify parties and nonparties, including employees of corporate or institutional
parties, of the contents of this
[Page 6 of 10]
-7-
paragraph. Failure to comply may lead to dismissal of claims, striking of defenses,
imposition of adverse inferences, or other consequences. Before any documents, data, and
tangible things which are reasonably calculated to lead to admissible evidence are
destroyed, altered, or erased, counsel shall confer to resolve questions as to whether the
information should be preserved. If counsel are unable to agree, any party may apply to this
Court for clarification or relief from this Order.

10. LOCAL CIVIL RULES, GENERALLY—Counsel shall familiarize themselves with this Court's
Local Rules and Guidelines. Except as provided herein to the contrary, the parties shall
comply with all such rules. The Court directs the parties' attention especially to the
requirements of D. Kan. Rule 5.1 (the procedure for filing pleadings and motions), D. Kan.
Rule 5.4 (the procedure for filing sealed documents), D. Kan. Rule 6.1(a) (motions for
extension of time to perform an act), D. Kan. Rule 37.1 (motions related to discovery); and
D. Kan. Rule 37.2 (the duty to confer regarding discovery disputes) as well as the Court's
ESI Guidelines and Deposition Guidelines. The Court waives the requirements of D. Kan. Rule
7.1(d)(2) and (d)(3) (page limitations for motions to dismiss, motions for summary judgment,
and class certification). The Court's Local Rules and Guidelines are available on this
Court's website, http://www.ksd.uscourts.gov.

11. HEARINGS—Hearings shall not be held on any motions, except by order of the Court upon
such notice as the Court may direct.

12. ANSWER DEADLINES STAYED—Defendants' obligations to answer or otherwise respond to the
initial complaints filed in these actions shall remain stayed pending the Initial Management
Conference and further order of the Court.

13. DISCOVERY PROCEEDINGS STAYED—All discovery proceedings in these actions are stayed until
further order of the Court, and the time requirements to perform any acts
[Page 7 of 10]
-8-
or file any papers pursuant to Rules 26 through 37 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure
are tolled until such time as a discovery schedule is established by order of the Court.

14. OTHER ORDERS REMAIN IN EFFECT—Unless modified by this Court, any orders, including
protective orders, previously entered by any transferor district court shall remain in full
force and effect.

Case Filing system ("CM/ECF"), with the Clerk of this Court and not with the transferor

19. NO LOCAL COUNSEL REQUIRED—No parties to any of these actions shall be required to obtain
local counsel in this district, and the requirements of D. Kan. Rule 83.5.4 are waived as to
any attorney appearing in these actions who is duly admitted to practice before any United
States District Court.

20. NOTICE OF TAG-ALONG CASES DIRECTLY FILED IN THIS DISTRICT—Defendants shall file a Notice
in the Master Docket whenever a case is filed in the District Court for the District of
Kansas that Defendants believe should be consolidated into this action, unless the action
already has been assigned to the undersigned judge.

21. INCORPORATION BY REFERENCE—Any paper filed herein that is substantially identical to any
other paper filed herein shall be sufficient if it incorporates by reference the paper to
which it is substantially identical. When counsel for more than one party plan to file
substantially identical documents, they must submit such documents jointly and file only one
document on behalf of all parties so joined.
